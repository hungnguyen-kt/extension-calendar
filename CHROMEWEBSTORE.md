## Store Listing

**Extension Name** [REQUIRED]
Calendar Extensions


**Short Description** [REQUIRED]
A dynamic and interactive Chrome extension to enhance calendar functionalities and take quick notes.


**Detailed Description** [REQUIRED]
Calendar Extensions is a beautifully designed, modern calendar that lives right in your browser toolbar. 

Features:
- View current and future months effortlessly with a clean, glassmorphic UI.
- Click on any day to write and save quick notes or reminders.
- A dynamic extension icon badge that always displays today's date.
- Smooth, buttery animations for a premium user experience.

How to use:
Simply click the extension icon to open your calendar. Navigate through months using the arrow buttons, or switch to the Year View to jump to a specific month. Click on any active day to add a note—days with notes will display a small dot indicator.

Privacy:
This extension respects your privacy. All your notes and settings are saved locally on your device using Chrome's local storage and are never transmitted to any external servers.
